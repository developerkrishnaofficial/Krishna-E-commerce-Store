About Krishna

Krishna is a homegrown fashion label built for people who love jackets that make a statement — bombers, varsity, leather and winter styles for both men and women.

Who We Are

We started Krishna with one simple goal: bring quality outerwear to every Indian wardrobe at honest prices, without compromising on style.

Quality First

Every jacket is checked for stitching, fabric and fit before it reaches you. We stand behind everything we sell.

Fast Delivery

We ship across India with reliable courier partners so your order reaches you quickly and safely.

Always Here For You

Questions about sizing, an order, or anything else? Our team is just an email away and happy to help.
